──────────────────────────────────────
Series Philosophy
──────────────────────────────────────

Little Heart Therapy Stories is a therapeutic picture book series based on narrative therapy.

The series does not teach children to eliminate fear.

Instead,

children learn that courage means taking one small step even while feeling afraid.

Every story follows these principles.

• The problem is the problem.

• Emotions are accepted, not eliminated.

• Children make their own choices.

• Small successes build confidence.

• Confidence develops through repeated experiences.

These principles remain unchanged throughout the series.

Story Pages
──────────────────────────────────────

Story pages present only the manuscript.

• one narrative step

──────────────────────────────────────
Activity Pages
──────────────────────────────────────

🏖️ Sand Play

🖍️ Drawing Activity

──────────────────────────────────────
Today's Courage Message
──────────────────────────────────────

Every volume includes one

Today's Courage Message

──────────────────────────────────────

• normalize emotions

# PART 2
# PAGE SPECIFICATIONS

──────────────────────────────────────
Purpose
──────────────────────────────────────

Every page type has a fixed purpose within the

Little Heart Therapy Stories

series.

The page sequence and page roles are defined in

SERIES_BIBLE.md.

This manual defines what each page is responsible for.

It does not change the page order.

──────────────────────────────────────
1. Cover
──────────────────────────────────────

Purpose

Introduce the new adventure.

The cover should immediately communicate

• the destination

• the emotional atmosphere

• the theme of this volume

The cover always includes

• Book title

• Book number

• Subtitle

The illustration should invite curiosity while maintaining a warm and reassuring feeling.

──────────────────────────────────────
2. Story Pages
──────────────────────────────────────

Purpose

Advance the story through one meaningful moment.

Each story page should contain

• one key event

• one emotional focus

• one step in the therapeutic journey

Story pages should never contain multiple major events.

The emotional progression should feel gradual and natural.

──────────────────────────────────────
3. Activity Pages
──────────────────────────────────────

Purpose

Transform the story into a therapeutic experience.

Every activity page contains

🏖️ Sand Play

🖍️ Drawing Activity

Activities should

• connect directly to the story

• encourage imagination

• encourage emotional expression

• avoid right or wrong answers

──────────────────────────────────────
4. Today's Courage Message
──────────────────────────────────────

Purpose

Help children remember the central therapeutic message.

This page contains one short encouraging sentence.

The message should

• be simple

• be memorable

• encourage courage

• encourage self-belief

The wording should be easy for young children to understand.

──────────────────────────────────────
5. Let's Play with Tori
──────────────────────────────────────

Purpose

Help children talk about their own experiences.

Reflection questions should

• encourage emotional awareness

• encourage personal storytelling

• encourage conversation with caregivers

Questions should never test memory or reading comprehension.

──────────────────────────────────────
6. Courage Map
──────────────────────────────────────

Purpose

Show the progress of Tori's journey.

Each new volume

• keeps all previously explored locations

• adds one newly explored destination

• leaves future destinations unexplored

The map should clearly communicate growth across the series.

──────────────────────────────────────
7. Next Adventure
──────────────────────────────────────

Purpose

Create gentle anticipation for the next volume.

Only introduce

• the next destination

Do not reveal

• important events

• solutions

• emotional outcomes

Curiosity should come from exploration, not danger.

──────────────────────────────────────
8. Message for Parents
──────────────────────────────────────

Purpose

Support parents in responding to their child's emotions.

The message should

• normalize fear

• praise courage

• encourage patience

• encourage emotional safety

Avoid

• criticism

• comparison

• pressure

The focus should always be on effort rather than results.

──────────────────────────────────────
9. Copyright Page
──────────────────────────────────────

Book 1 only.

Generate the copyright page only for the first volume.

Books 2–6 reuse the same copyright page during final EPUB production.

Do not regenerate or modify the copyright page after Book 1.

──────────────────────────────────────
Page Consistency
──────────────────────────────────────

Every page type has one fixed role throughout the series.

Readers should immediately recognize each page type regardless of the book number.

The reading experience should remain familiar, predictable and emotionally safe across all volumes.
